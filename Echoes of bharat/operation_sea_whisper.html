FIELD GUIDE · OLIVE RIDLEY

KNOW YOUR SUBJECT

A good field observer knows what to look for before making a report.

01 · NESTING Female Olive Ridley turtles come ashore to dig nests in sandy beaches and lay their eggs.
02 · NEST CHAMBER The female uses her hind flippers to create a chamber in the sand where the eggs are deposited.
03 · ODISHA Odisha's coast contains some of the world's most important Olive Ridley nesting beaches.
04 · ARRIBADA Large numbers of Olive Ridleys can gather to nest during spectacular mass-nesting events called arribadas.
05 · FIELD RULE Observe first. Avoid touching, moving or unnecessarily disturbing a nesting turtle or its nest.
