The Story

A temple built for the Sun

The Konark Sun Temple was conceived as a monumental representation of the Sun God's chariot. Its architecture connects mythology, astronomy, time and craftsmanship into one extraordinary monument.


The temple was built in the 13th century during the reign of King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty. The monument was designed as the celestial chariot of Surya, the Sun God.
