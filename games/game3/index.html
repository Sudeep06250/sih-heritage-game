The Legend of Chhena Poda

Originating in Nayagarh, Odisha, Chhena Poda ("Baked Cheese") was invented by Sudarsan Sahoo in the mid-20th century. By leaving caramelised cottage cheese, sugar, and cashews overnight in a dying charcoal oven, he created Odisha’s signature roasted dessert!

YOU LOST

Whispers say that beneath the shadow of Konark’s Sun Temple, an ancient artisan attempted to bake a caramelized offering of cottage cheese to appease the sun deity before the final crown stone was set. But the recipe was corrupted, and the offering burned into ash. As night fell, the sea roared, taking the master architect’s son into its dark depths—leaving only the scorched, bitter scent of burnt chhena lingering in the hollow temple walls forever...
